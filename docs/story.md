## Quick Reference

- **Module 3** of the Cohort Protocol — the final practical. **Real:** you are awake aboard the *Longwatch*, in flight, doing the genetic testing and the rest. You are locked in.
- **Contingency trained:** no landfall — the ship is everything you have, and you cannot expand it.
- **CASPAR tone:** technical, scientific.
- **Cohort:** always `CP-Verdant-S1`. This is **Module 3 · CP Influx**.

## The ship

Influx's ship **is** the real *Longwatch*, in flight, and you are awake aboard it for the final practical. That makes the navigation-directive fragment (see [The Navigation Fragment](https://github.com/SpecterRealm/minecraft-modpack-cp-liminal/blob/main/docs/series/story.md#the-navigation-fragment)) land naturally in Influx's late leaks, and it connects the road: the ship that confines you here is the ship whose course correction sends you down to Liminal.

## Opening narrative *(draft)*

There is no world to land on.

There is the ship, and there is what is aboard it. Every wall is a wall. Every room is the last room. When the hull is full, it is full — you cannot pour a foundation, claim another hill, or build another floor.

CASPAR describes the third module as the simplest and the hardest: nothing is wasted, because nothing can be replaced. Debris becomes matter. Matter becomes patterns. Patterns become life. When you need room, you do not build outward — you make the space you have hold more.

Learn to make everything from what you have.
